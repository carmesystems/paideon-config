Paideon — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Paideon mobile app (Android, com.carmesystems.paideon) and the Paideon website · Effective 22 November 2026 · Updated 26 November 2026 (sign-in records)

In short: Paideon is used by the staff of institutions that have chosen it (for example colleges and research centres). Your institution decides what is recorded and keeps the data on its own Paideon server. Carme Systems runs Paideon for the institution. We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and we do not use it for any purpose other than running Paideon for your institution.

1. Who we are

Paideon is built and operated by Carme Systems (“we”). We provide Paideon to institutions (“your institution”), such as a college for staff leave or a research centre for a study.

Your institution decides why and how personal information is used, and is responsible for it (under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, it is the Data Fiduciary). Carme Systems processes the information only on your institution’s instructions.

2. Who can use the app

Only people to whom an institution has given a login. There is no public sign-up. Each phone is registered to its user by the institution’s office before use. The app is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

4. Permissions on your phone

  • Camera: to scan your office’s set-up QR code and to take photos you choose to attach.
  • Location: only for field trips and visits you start; you can refuse it and still use the rest of the app.
  • Notifications: approvals, reminders and a notice while a field trip is running.
  • Fingerprint / face: the app lock, checked by Android itself. Your fingerprint or face never leaves your phone; Paideon receives only “unlocked”.

5. Where it is kept and who sees it

  • Information goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to your institution’s own Paideon server. It is not stored on the phone longer than needed.
  • Inside Paideon, people see only what their role allows: for example, your approvers see your leave, and a study’s team sees its participants. Participants’ names and contact details are kept encrypted, apart from the study answers.
  • Carme Systems staff access a server only to run, support or repair it for your institution.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Services used to run Paideon: Google (Play Integrity, to check the app is genuine); messages by SMS, WhatsApp or e-mail through the institution’s providers; and translation services (Bhashini, Government of India; Microsoft Azure) for the wording of questionnaires.
  • Information may be disclosed when the law requires it.

6. How long it is kept

For as long as your institution needs it for its records (for example, service and leave records) or for the research study under its approval, and then deleted or anonymised under your institution’s rules. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction, deletion: ask your institution’s office (for staff) or the study team (for research participants). They can see, correct, or remove your information, and deactivate your login and registered phones. Some records may have to be kept where the law or the institution’s rules require.
  • Withdrawing consent (research): participants may withdraw from a study at any time through the study team.
  • On your phone: you can turn off location, camera or notifications in Android settings, or uninstall the app; this removes the app’s data from the phone.
  • If your institution cannot help, write to us (below) and we will pass your request to it and help it respond.

8. Security

Encrypted connections; logins tied to registered phones; passwords stored only as secure hashes; role-based access; an audit trail of changes; and regular backups.
